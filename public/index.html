Lists & lookups

    
Complete lists come from official registers; specific businesses and posts can be looked up directly.

    
🏛️ Government & official lists — import, match, make leads
      
Import public lists published by government bodies, then every lead (from new runs, or with the
        button below) is matched to its official record: the AI compares name (spelling variants, Pvt/Ltd, initials),
        place, type and any registration no. A confirmed match adds the record's registered phone / email / website /
        address to the lead; weaker ones are shown as “possible” for you to check. Good sources: MCA company / LLP master
        data, state transport lists of motor-driving schools, NCVT / Skill India ITI & training-partner lists, Indian
        Nursing Council institutions, Udyam lists, any data.gov.in dataset. Voter rolls, Aadhaar, e-Shram and similar ID
        lists are refused (not public; illegal to use for outreach).

      

        

        

      

      

        
        

      

        

      

        

      

        📚 Imported lists → ➕ Make leads: every business of the list in that region becomes a lead, and its phone / email / website / owners are looked up.

      

        
        

      

    

    
🏢 Find specific businesses by name
      
Type business names (one per line, up to 8 at a time) — each is looked up on Google Maps (phone, website,
        address), its own website is read (phones, emails, WhatsApp, people) and its owners / directors are searched on LinkedIn.

      
      

        

    

    
🔗 Analyse specific posts / pages (paste links)
      
Paste Instagram / Facebook / YouTube / Reddit / blog / forum links, one per line. Each page is read
        directly (no searching) and every commenter showing interest becomes a lead. Instagram: the public embed page shows
        the caption and usually only the first few comments without login, and it needs “Respect robots.txt” unticked. All
        comments of your own posts come through the 🤖 auto-reply (Meta API).

      
      

        Uses the discovery intent above (or “people interested in this post” if empty).

    

    
🔎 Search everything saved (meaning, words and misspelt names)
      
Finds saved leads, candidates and government-list records by meaning (“heavy vehicle trainer” finds
        “HMV driving instructor”), exact words and registration numbers, and misspelt names — pgvector + full-text + fuzzy
        search on your Neon database. Run “Update search index” once after upgrading, then new leads are indexed automatically.

      

        
        
        
        

      

    

  


  

    
Outreach

    

    

      🤖 Auto-reply to “interested” comments on your own Instagram / Facebook posts
      
When someone comments “interested” (or similar) on a post from your official Instagram
        Business account or Facebook Page, the tool replies under the comment, sends them a private message asking
        for their name, WhatsApp, email and experience, and saves what they reply as a candidate — through Meta’s
        official API. Only your own posts: Meta does not allow automated replies on other people’s posts.

Zero-send check: syntax, disposable inbox, DNS / MX,
        then the mail server is asked (SMTP RCPT) whether the mailbox exists — nothing is sent. “Catch-all” means the
        domain accepts every address, so the answer is inconclusive. Vercel blocks port 25; set SMTP_VERIFY_URL
        (email_verify.py running on any small server) or a Hunter / ZeroBounce / NeverBounce key for mailbox checks.

🛡️ Privacy & consent

    
People who opt out (STOP reply to the auto-reply, 🚫 on a lead, or added here) are never saved or contacted
      again, and their saved contact details are erased. Individuals' contact details can be erased after a retention period;
      businesses are kept. Every contact keeps where it came from (source link and contact source).
