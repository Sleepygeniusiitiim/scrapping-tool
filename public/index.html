      📥 Import candidates from Naukri / foundit / WorkIndia / Indeed / Apna (employer-account export)
      
In your employer account, download the applicants or database
        search results as Excel / CSV (Naukri Resdex / RMS: select candidates → Download / Export to Excel;
        foundit, Indeed, Apna, WorkIndia: applicants → Download / Export). Then upload the file here — names, phone
        numbers, emails, roles, skills and locations are saved with the other leads. The tool never logs in to
        these portals with your password: automated logins break their terms and get recruiter accounts blocked.

      

        

        

        

        

      

      

    

    

  

  

    

      🤖 Auto-reply to “interested” comments on your own Instagram / Facebook posts
      
When someone comments “interested” (or similar) on a post from your official Instagram
        Business account or Facebook Page, the tool replies under the comment, sends them a private message asking
        for their name, WhatsApp, email and experience, and saves what they reply as a candidate — through Meta’s
        official API. Only your own posts: Meta does not allow automated replies on other people’s posts.
